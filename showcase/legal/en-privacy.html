1. Overview

This Policy sets out the data processing practices for users of the interactive property selection service provided by Deniz Estate, under the EU GDPR (Regulation EU 2016/679) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD).

3. Categories of Data Collected

We collect only the following details via our quiz:

  • Name for communication;
  • Channel: WhatsApp or Telegram;
  • Phone number with country code or Telegram username;
  • Property criteria: purpose (investment, living, holiday, Golden Visa), property type, preferred locations, budget and timeframe;
  • Technical consent records: timestamp, session ID, country code.

We never ask for passports, bank accounts or payment cards through this form.

4. Purpose and Legal Basis

Data is processed on the basis of:

  • Your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Art. 6 LOPDGDD). Submitting the form requires consent;
  • Pre-contractual measures (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): preparing tailored property selections and consultations.

Consent does not constitute a marketing subscription.

5. Data Security and Confidentiality

Submissions are encrypted via TLS/HTTPS and stored securely. Only authorised Deniz Estate property specialists have access under strict confidentiality. Data is never sold or shared with unauthorised third parties.

6. Retention period

The retention period and deletion procedure must be confirmed by the data controller before this service goes live. Automatic deletion is not configured in the current test version.

7. Your GDPR Rights

Under Articles 15, 22 GDPR, you have the right to request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, and withdrawal of consent. To exercise your rights, email info@deniz-estate.com with the subject “Personal Data”. You also have the right to complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency: Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD) (aepd.es).
